The Golden Rule

Name & Role!

Every noun has a specific name and a grammatical role in a sentence:
• Proper Noun: Always starts with a Capital Letter (Tom, France, Madrid).
• Abstract Noun: Cannot be touched or seen, only felt or understood (beauty, courage, joy).
• Plural Traps: Calf → Calves, Piano → Pianos, Sheep → Sheep.
• Uncountables: Never say furnitures or an advice!

Unit 01 · Fundamentals

1. Kinds & Functions of Nouns (संज्ञा के प्रकार और कार्य)

A noun is the name of a person, place, animal, thing, quality, or group. Learn the 4 kinds and 5 sentence functions.

A. Four Kinds of Nouns in English

Core Concept
👉 सरल समझ (Hindi Explanation):
1. Common Noun (जातिवाचक संज्ञा): किसी भी साधारण वस्तु, व्यक्ति, प्राणी या स्थान का आम नाम — dog, man, table, boy, school, city.
2. Proper Noun (व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा): किसी विशेष व्यक्ति, स्थान, दिन या महीने का निजी नाम। यह हमेशा बड़े अक्षर (Capital Letter) से शुरू होता है — France, Madrid, Mrs Smith, Tom, India, Monday.
3. Abstract Noun (भाववाचक संज्ञा): ऐसा गुण, भाव, विचार या अवस्था जिसे हम छू या देख नहीं सकते, केवल समझ और महसूस कर सकते हैं — beauty, charity, courage, fear, joy, honesty.
4. Collective Noun (समूहवाचक संज्ञा): व्यक्तियों, प्राणियों या वस्तुओं के पूरे समूह या झुंड को एक इकाई मानने वाला नाम — crowd, flock, group, swarm, team, herd, fleet.
Common: dog, man, table आम जातिगत प्राणी या वस्तु का नाम।
Proper: Tom, France, Madrid विशेष नाम, हमेशा Capital Letter से शुरू।
Abstract: beauty, courage, joy गुण, भाव या अवस्था, जिसे छू नहीं सकते।
Collective: crowd, flock, swarm, team व्यक्तियों या प्राणियों का पूरा समूह।

Unit 02 · Gender Rules

2. Gender of Nouns (लिंग)

Gender indicates whether a noun is male, female, inanimate (non-living), or common to both sexes.

A. Four Main Gender Categories

• Masculine (पुल्लिंग): Men, boys, and male animals (Pronoun: he / they).
• Feminine (स्त्रीलिंग): Women, girls, and female animals (Pronoun: she / they).
• Neuter (नपुंसकलिंग): Inanimate objects (निर्जीव वस्तुएँ जैसे table, book), and animals or infants whose sex is unknown (Pronoun: it / they).
• Common Gender (उभयलिंग): Nouns that can refer to either male or female (baby, child, cousin, infant, parent, doctor, artist).

Special Exceptions: Affectionate Feminine (Ships & Countries)

Exam Favorite

In English, inanimate things or geographical entities are treated as Feminine (she / her) in two special situations:

1. Ships (and sometimes cars): When spoken of with affection or respect, ships take feminine pronouns:
“The ship struck an iceberg, which tore a huge hole in her side.” (जहाज हिमखंड से टकराया जिससे उसकी बगल में बड़ा छेद हो गया।)

2. Countries by Name: When a country's courage, history, or people are referred to:
“Scotland lost many of her bravest men in two great rebellions.” (स्कॉटलैंड ने अपने कई बहादुर सपूत खो दिए।)

Unit 03 · Plural Formations

3. Plural Formations (एकवचन से बहुवचन)

Rules for forming plurals, pronunciation sound patterns, and creatures that never change.

Rule 1: General Rule — Adding '-s' & Sound Rules

Pronunciation
• The plural of a noun is usually made by adding -s to the singular: day → days, dog → dogs, house → houses.
• ध्वनि का नियम: यदि शब्द p, k, f ध्वनि पर समाप्त हो, तो '-s' का उच्चारण /s/ निकलता है (caps, books, cliffs)। अन्य ध्वनियों के बाद /z/ निकलता है (dogs, days)।
• जब '-s' को ce, ge, se, ze के बाद लगाते हैं, तो उच्चारण में एक नया शब्दांश /ɪz/ (इज़) जुड़ जाता है (houses, places, roses, pages).

Rule 2: Nouns ending in -o, -ch, -sh, -ss, -x (+es vs +s)

Trap Alert
👉 Add '-es': सीटी या फुफकार जैसी ध्वनि (hissing sound) वाले शब्दों में -es लगता है:
• tomato → tomatoes • church → churches • brush → brushes • kiss → kisses • box → boxes.

⚠️ Foreign Origin & Abbreviation Exception: विदेशी या संक्षिप्त शब्दों के अंत में '-o' होने पर केवल '-s' जुड़ता है, '-es' नहीं:
• dynamo → dynamos • kilo → kilos • kimono → kimonos
• photo → photos • piano → pianos • soprano → sopranos

Rule 3: Words ending in '-y' (Consonant+y vs Vowel+y)

Consonant + y → drop 'y' and add '-ies' baby → babies, country → countries, fly → flies, lady → ladies. ('y' से ठीक पहले व्यंजन r, d, l, b है!)
Vowel + y → keep 'y' and add '-s' boy → boys, day → days, donkey → donkeys, guy → guys, monkey → monkeys. ('y' से ठीक पहले स्वर है!)

Rule 4: Nouns ending in '-f' or '-fe' (The Twelve Words & Exceptions)

High Yield

English has Twelve Nouns that drop 'f' or 'fe' and take '-ves' in the plural:

Rule 5: Irregular Vowel Changes & Unchanged Creatures

👉 आंतरिक स्वर बदलकर बहुवचन:
• foot → feet • goose → geese • louse → lice • man → men
• mouse → mice • tooth → teeth • woman → women
• Special additions: child → children, ox → oxen.

👉 Creatures that DO NOT CHANGE (Singular = Plural):
• sheep (भेड़): one sheep, two sheep (कभी 'sheeps' न लिखें ✗)
• deer (हिरण): one deer, five deer (कभी 'deers' न लिखें ✗)
• fish (मछली): सामान्यतः fish ही रहता है। न बदलने वाली मछलियाँ: carp, pike, salmon, trout, cod, plaice, squid, turbot, mackerel.
• Marine creatures taking '-s': crabs, herrings, sardines, eels, lobsters, sharks.
• Game birds (hunters): duck, partridge, pheasant (शिकार के संदर्भ में रूप नहीं बदलता)। 'game' शब्द हमेशा एकवचन क्रिया लेता है।
• Other unchanged words: aircraft, craft, quid, counsel.

Unit 04 · Traps & Agreement

4. Plural Traps, Agreement & Compounds

Nouns that are always plural, plural-looking singular nouns, and compound noun pluralization.

A. Words that are ALWAYS PLURAL (Take a Plural Verb)

Plural Verb Required
• Clothes and Police: The police are investigating. (Never 'police is'!)
• Garments of two parts (दो टांगों वाले वस्त्र): breeches, pants, pyjamas, trousers, shorts, jeans.
• Tools of two parts (दो फलक/ब्लेड वाले औज़ार): binoculars (दूरबीन), glasses/spectacles (चश्मा), pliers (पिलास), scales (तराजू), scissors (कैंची), shears (कतरनी).
• इन्हें गिनने के लिए 'a pair of' लगाते हैं: a pair of scissors, two pairs of trousers.
• Other Plural Nouns: arms (हथियार), damages (हर्जाना), earnings (कमाई), goods (सामान), greens (सब्जियाँ), grounds, outskirts (बाहरी इलाका), pains (कठिन प्रयास), particulars, premises (परिसर), riches (दौलत), savings (बचत), stairs (सीढ़ियाँ), surroundings, valuables (कीमती वस्तुएँ).

C. Foreign Plurals & Compound Nouns Rules

👉 Greek & Latin Plurals:
• crisis → crises • erratum → errata • memorandum → memoranda
• oasis → oases • phenomenon → phenomena • radius → radii • terminus → termini.
• English rules: dogma → dogmas, gymnasium → gymnasiums, formula → formulas.
• Two Plurals with Different Meanings:
- appendix: appendixes (चिकित्सा संबंधी) अथवा appendices (पुस्तक का परिशिष्ट)।
- index: indexes (पुस्तक की अनुक्रमणिका) अथवा indices (गणितीय सूचकांक)।

👉 Compound Nouns Pluralization:
1. Normally the last word is plural: boy-friends, break-ins, travel agents.
2. When prefixed by 'man' or 'woman', both parts are made plural: men drivers, women drivers, men teachers!
3. Prepositions or adverbs: first word is plural: runners-up, hangers-on, sisters-in-law, ladies-in-waiting.
4. Abbreviations / Initials: MPs, VIPs, UFOs.

D. Collective Nouns Agreement (Team is vs Team are)

Collective nouns like crew, family, team, jury, government can take either verb:
• Singular Verb: When thought of as a single united body — “Our team is the best.” (पूरी टीम एक इकाई के रूप में।)
• Plural Verb: When thought of as individual members — “Our team are wearing their new jerseys.” (खिलाड़ी अपनी-अपनी जर्सी पहन रहे हैं।)
• Possessive pronoun: their is more common than its: “The jury are considering their verdict.”

Unit 05 · Mass Nouns

5. Uncountable Nouns & Partitives (अगणनीय संज्ञा)

Nouns that cannot be counted directly, their rules, and partitive counters used to quantify them.

A. Common Uncountable Nouns in English

Never with a/an
1. Substances (पदार्थ व धातु): bread, cream, gold, paper, tea, beer, dust, ice, sand, wafer, cloth, gin, jam, soap, wine, coffee, glass, oil, stone, wood.
2. Abstract Concepts (भाववाचक संज्ञाएँ): advice, beauty, courage, death, experience, fear, help, hope, horror, information, knowledge, mercy, pity, relief, suspicion, work.
3. Strictly Uncountable in English: baggage, camping, damage, furniture, luggage, parking, shopping, weather, hair, rubbish, news.

B. Partitive Counters: How to Count Uncountables

Must Learn

Never place 'a/an' or plural '-s' directly with uncountable nouns. Use these standard partitive counters:

C. Dual Countable & Uncountable Senses

• Hair (बाल): सिर के पूरे बाल सामूहिक रूप से अगणनीय हैं (Her hair is black)। परंतु अलग-अलग संख्या में गिनने पर गणनीय हैं (She has two grey hairs)।
• Drinks (Coffee, Tea, Beer): तरल रूप में अगणनीय हैं। परंतु कप या ग्लास के आर्डर के रूप में गणनीय बन जाते हैं (Can I have a coffee and two teas, please?)।
• Glass: सामग्री (शीशा) के रूप में अगणनीय है। पानी पीने के बर्तन के रूप में गणनीय (a glass of water) तथा चश्मे के लिए glasses.
• Wood: लकड़ी के रूप में अगणनीय है। जंगल के अर्थ में woods कहा जाता है (a walk in the woods)।
• Experience: संचित अनुभव के रूप में अगणनीय (5 years of experience)। परंतु रोमांचक घटना के लिए गणनीय (an exciting experience)।
• Work: रोज़गार/कार्य के रूप में अगणनीय (looking for work)। सड़क मरम्मत को roadworks और साहित्यिक कृतियों को Shakespeare's complete works कहते हैं।

D. Abstract Nouns with 'a/an' in Particular Senses

कुछ भाववाचक संज्ञाएँ किसी विशिष्ट सहायता, घटना या स्थिति को दर्शाने पर एकवचन में 'a/an' ले सकती हैं:
• a help: “My children are a great help to me.” / “A good map would be a help.”
• a relief: “It was a relief to sit down.” (बैठना एक बड़ा सुकून था।)
• a knowledge of: “He has a good knowledge of mathematics.”
• a love / hatred / horror of: “a love of music”, “a hatred of violence”.
• a pity / shame: “It's a pity you couldn't come.”
• a fear / hope / suspicion: “There is a fear that he was injured.”
