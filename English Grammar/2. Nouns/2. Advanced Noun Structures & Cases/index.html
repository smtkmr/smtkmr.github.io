Unit 01 · Morphology

1. The Form of Possessive / Genitive Case ('s and ')

Understand which nouns take 's and which take only an apostrophe (') with simple explanations.

Rule 1: Singular Nouns and Irregular Plurals not ending in 's' take "'s"

Basic Foundation
👉 सरल नियम (Simple Rule): जब कोई noun एकवचन (singular) हो, या फिर ऐसा बहुवचन (irregular plural) हो जो 's' पर समाप्त नहीं होता (जैसे men, women, children, people), तो हमेशा 's लगाते हैं!
a man's job, a woman's intuition
Singular noun + 's (आदमी का काम, महिला की सहज बुद्धि)
men's work, women's clothes
Irregular plural (men/women) without 's' + 's (पुरुषों का काम, महिलाओं के कपड़े)
a child's voice, the children's room
Child (singular) vs Children (irregular plural) (बच्चे की आवाज़, बच्चों का कमरा)
the horse's mouth, the bull's horns
Animals with 's (घोड़े का मुँह, साँड के सींग)
the people's choice, the crew's quarters
Collective / Irregular plural + 's (जनता की पसंद, चालक दल का कमरा)
Russia's exports, India's culture
Countries with 's (रूस का निर्यात, भारत की संस्कृति)

Rule 2: Plural Nouns ending in 's' add ONLY an apostrophe (')

High Yield
👉 सरल नियम (Rule): अगर कोई शब्द पहले से बहुवचन (plural) है और उसके अंत में 's' लगा है (जैसे girls, students, eagles, Smiths), तो दोबारा 's मत लगाएँ! सिर्फ शब्द के अंत में एक अपोस्ट्रॉफी ' (apostrophe) लगा दें।
⇄ Swipe table horizontally on mobile (मोबाइल पर पूरी टेबल देखने के लिए खिसकाएं)
Plural Noun Incorrect (✗) Correct (✓) Hindi Meaning / Explanation
Girls a girls's school ✗ a girls' school ✓ लड़कियों का स्कूल (केवल ' जुड़ेगा)
Students the students's hostel ✗ the students' hostel ✓ छात्रों का छात्रावास (केवल ' जुड़ेगा)
Eagles the eagles's nest ✗ the eagles' nest ✓ चीलों का घोंसला (केवल ' जुड़ेगा)
The Smiths the Smiths's car ✗ the Smiths' car ✓ स्मिथ परिवार की कार (केवल ' जुड़ेगा)

Rule 3: Classical Names vs Modern Names ending in 's'

Exam Special
👉 A. Classical / Historical Names (प्राचीन यूनानी व ऐतिहासिक नाम): Pythagoras, Archimedes, Sophocles जैसे प्राचीन शास्त्रीय नामों में केवल अपोस्ट्रॉफी (') जुड़ता है:
• Pythagoras' Theorem (पाइथागोरस का प्रमेय)
• Archimedes' Law (आर्किमिडीज का सिद्धांत)
• Sophocles' plays (सोफोक्लीज के नाटक)

👉 B. Modern Names ending in 's' (आधुनिक नाम): आधुनिक नामों में आप 's या केवल ' दोनों में से कोई भी लगा सकते हैं (दोनों रूप मान्य हैं):
• Mr Jones's house (or Mr Jones' house)
• Yeats's poems (or Yeats' poems)

Rule 4: Compound Nouns, Titles, Initials, and Disappearance of 'the'

Master Rule
👉 A. Compound Nouns & Titles: 's हमेशा अंतिम शब्द (last word) पर लगता है:
• my brother-in-law's guitar (NOT brother's-in-law ✗)
• Henry the Eighth's wives / the Prince of Wales's helicopter
• Initials: the PM's secretary, the MP's briefcase, the VIP's escort.

👉 B. Disappearance of Article 'the' (अति-महत्वपूर्ण!): जब 'of' को हटाकर possessive 's लगाते हैं, तो अधिकार वाली वस्तु से पहले का 'the' हट जाता है:
• the daughter of the politician = the politician's daughter (NOT the politician's the daughter ✗)
• the intervention of America = America's intervention
• the plays of Shakespeare = Shakespeare's plays

Unit 02 · Usage Patterns

2. Use of Possessive ('s) vs "of + noun"

Learn when to use possessive 's and when the 'of + noun' construction is preferred or safer.

A. Living Beings (People, Animals, Countries) vs Inanimate Objects

Possessive 's is chiefly used of people, countries, or animals:

• सजीव संज्ञाएं (Living beings): the boy's dog, the doctor's car, India's economy.
• निर्जीव वस्तुएं (Inanimate things): इनके साथ 'of + noun' का प्रयोग होता है — the walls of the town, the roof of the church, the keys of the car.
• Noun + Noun विकल्प: इन्हें अक्सर दो nouns जोड़कर भी लिखा जाता है: the town walls, the church roof, the car keys! यहाँ पहला noun विशेषण का काम करता है और कभी plural नहीं बनता (the roofs of the churches = the church roofs).

B. When the possessor noun is followed by a phrase or clause → "of" is required!

Crucial Rule

When a descriptive phrase or clause follows the possessor, use 'of' instead of 's:

अगर संज्ञा के तुरंत बाद कोई लंबा वाक्यांश (phrase या clause) जुड़ा हो, तो 's नहीं लगाते, बल्कि 'of' लगाते हैं:
• The boys ran about, obeying the directions of a man with a whistle.
(NOT: a man with a whistle's directions ✗ — सीटी के आगे 's लगाने से अर्थ बदल जाएगा!)
• I took the advice of a couple I met in the train and hired a car.

C. Ships, Vehicles, Time Expressions, and Idiomatic Phrases

Ships and Boats the ship's bell, the yacht's mast (जहाज और नाव में 's पूरी तरह मानक है)
Vehicles (Planes, Trains, Cars) a glider's wings OR the wings of a glider; the train's heating OR heating system of the train ('of' अधिक सुरक्षित है)
Time Expressions a week's holiday, today's paper, tomorrow's weather, ten minutes' break (or a ten-minute break)
Money + worth £1's worth of stamps, ten dollars' worth of ice-cream
For + noun + sake for heaven's sake, for goodness' sake ('s' पर समाप्त होने के कारण केवल ' लगता है)
Idiomatic Expressions a stone's throw (बहुत नज़दीक / थोड़ी दूरी पर), journey's end (यात्रा का अंत), the water's edge (पानी का किनारा)

D. Seasons Rule (Winter, Summer, Spring, Autumn)

• Winter / Summer: हम a winter's day या a winter day, और a summer's day या a summer day दोनों कह सकते हैं।
• Spring / Autumn: इनके साथ 's नहीं लगता! केवल तब लगता है जब साहित्य में इनका मानवीकरण (personified) किया गया हो: Autumn's return (पतझड़ की वापसी)।

Unit 03 · Compound Nouns

3. Compound Nouns (प्रकार और संयोजन)

Combining two words to form a new noun — types, relationships, and important exceptions.

A. Three Structural Types of Compound Nouns

3 Patterns
1. Noun + Noun London Transport, kitchen table, petrol tank, river bank, hall door, traffic warden, winter clothes.
2. Noun + Gerund (-ing) fruit picking, lorry driving, coal-mining, weight-lifting, bird-watching, surf-riding.
3. Gerund (-ing) + Noun waiting list, diving-board, driving licence, landing card, dining-room, swimming pool.

Unit 04 · Advanced Nuances

4. Advanced Possessive Uses & Pronunciation Stress

Omitted nouns for shops, clinics, homes, and where to place voice stress in English speech.

A. Omitted Second Noun (Shops, Offices, Surgeries, Homes)

Daily English
अक्सर बोलचाल में दूसरा संज्ञा शब्द (shop, office, surgery या house) छोड़ दिया जाता है:

• the baker's / the butcher's / the chemist's / the florist's = bakery, butcher shop, chemist shop, florist shop.
• the travel agent's / the house agent's = travel office, real estate office.
• the dentist's / the doctor's / the vet's = dentist's surgery, doctor's clinic, vet's surgery.
Example: "You can buy this medicine at the chemist's." / "He is going to the dentist's."
• Homes: "We had lunch at Bill's" (= Bill's house). / "We met at Ann's" (= Ann's house).
• Famous Shops: Sotheby's, Claridge's keep the 's; while Harrods, Foyles drop the apostrophe.
