Purpose Decides!

Unit 01 · 6. The Definite Article

6. the (The Definite Article)

Definite article 'the' singular, plural aur sabhi genders ke liye same rehta hai.

A. Form: 'the' Sabhi Genders aur Numbers ke liye ek samaan hai

Form & Nature
👉 No Gender/Number Change: English me 'the' ka roop kabhi nahi badalta chahe noun singular ho ya plural, masculine ho ya feminine:
the boy / the boys Masculine Singular & Plural
the girl / the girls Feminine Singular & Plural
the day / the days Neuter Singular & Plural

B. Core Uses of 'the'

Essential Rules
Definite article the ka prayog in situations me hota hai:

1. Unique Objects (jo sansaar me anokhe ya akele hon):
• the earth, the sea, the sky, the equator, the sun, the moon, the stars.

2. Second Mention (jab kisi noun ka doosri baar zikr ho):
• Pehli baar: His car struck a tree.
• Doosri baar: You can still see the mark on the tree. (Ab tree specific ho gaya hai)

3. Noun made definite by a phrase or clause (vishesh vivaran aane par):
• the girl in blue (neele kapdo wali ladki)
• the man with the banner / the boy that I met / the place where I met him.

4. Locality / Immediate Context (jis sthan ya context me sirf wahi ek cheez ho):
• Ann is in the garden. (= is ghar ka garden)
• Please pass the wine. (= mez par rakhi hui wine)
• the postman (= hamare yahan aane wala postman), the car (= hamari car), the newspaper (= jo akhbaar hum padhte hain).

5. Superlatives, Ordinals (first, second...) aur "only":
• the first week, the second attempt, the best day, the highest mountain, the only way.

C. 'the + singular noun' representing a whole class

Class vs Man
• The whale is in danger of becoming extinct. (= Saari whales ki jaati)
• The deep-freeze has made life easier for housewives.
• The small shopkeeper is finding life increasingly difficult.
• Yeh singular verb aur pronouns (he, she, it) leta hai: The first-class traveller pays more so he expects some comfort.
⚠️ CRITICAL EXCEPTION — 'MAN':
Jab man ka use poori manav jati (human race) ke liye ho, to uske aage koi article NAHI lagta:
• If oil supplies run out, man may have to fall back on the horse. (NOT: the man ✗)
• Man is mortal. (NOT: The man is mortal ✗)

D. 'the + adjective' representing a class of persons

Jab kisi adjective ke aage 'the' lagakar us varg ke sabhi logon ko darshaya jaye, to yeh plural noun ban jata hai aur plural verb leta hai:

• the old = old people in general (vridhh log)
• the poor = poor people in general (gareeb log) → The poor are suffering.
• the rich, the blind, the unemployed, the young.

E. Proper Names: Kahan 'the' Lagta Hai aur Kahan Nahi

High Yield Exam Rules

Geographical names me 'the' ka use bohot specific hota hai. Neeche di gayi table dhyan se padhein:

⇄ Swipe table horizontally on mobile (मोबाइल पर पूरी टेबल देखने के लिए खिसकाएं)
Category 'the' ke saath (Correct ✓) Bina 'the' ke (No Article ✗) Explanation / Trick
Oceans, Seas & Rivers the Atlantic, the Thames, the Ganges, the Pacific Lake Victoria, Lake Superior Nadi, samudra aur mahasagar ke aage 'the' lagta hai; lekin akele 'Lake' ke aage nahi lagta.
Mountain Chains vs Single Peaks the Alps, the Himalayas, the Rockies Mount Everest ✗ (NOT the Everest), Mont Blanc, K2 Pahaadi shrameela (mountain ranges) me 'the' lagega; akele parvat shikhar (single peak) me NAHI!
Islands the Azores, the West Indies, the Bahamas, the Andamans Sicily, Sri Lanka, Java Dweep samooh (group of islands) me 'the' lagega; akele dweep (single island) me nahi.
Deserts & Regions the Sahara, the Crimea, the Riviera, the Kalahari Central Asia, Siberia Registan aur pramukh bhaugolik kshetron ke aage 'the' lagta hai.
Plural / Republic Countries the Netherlands, the United States, the Philippines India, France, Germany, Japan Plural naam wale ya 'Republic/Kingdom/States' wale deshon ke aage 'the' lagta hai.
Noun + of + Noun the Bay of Biscay, the Gulf of Mexico, the Cape of Good Hope Hudson Bay 'Noun + of + Noun' pattern me hamesha 'the' lagta hai.
Directions (Disha) the North Pole, the East End, the north of Spain, the Middle East South Africa, North America, West Germany Single geographical deshon me 'South Africa' bolte hain; par noun form me 'the north of Spain'.
👉 Compass Direction: Adverb vs Noun:
• Go north (Adverb: uttar disha me jao — NO article)
• He lives in the north (Noun: uttar ke kshetra me rehta hai — 'the' aayega).

F & G. Monuments, Newspapers, Pop Groups & People's Names

Monuments & Buildings the National Gallery, the Tower of London, the British Museum
Choirs, Bands & Orchestras the Bach Choir, the Philadelphia Orchestra, the Beatles
Newspapers & Ships The Times, The Hindu, the Great Britain, the Titanic
the + Plural Surname = The Family the Smiths = Mr and Mrs Smith and their children.
• Distinguishing a person: "We have two Mr Smiths. Which do you want?" ~ "I want the Mr Smith who signed this letter."
• Titles with 'of': the Duke of York, the Queen of England. (Lekin rank ke saath naam aane par nahi: Lord Olivier, Captain Cook; par akele rank me: The captain ordered...)
• Joint address: The Misses Smith (dono behno ko ek saath patra likhte waqt).

Unit 02 · 7. Omission Rules

7. Omission of "the"

Kahan-kahan definite article 'the' lagana sakht mana hai.

1. Abstract Nouns: General Sense vs Specific Sense

Exam Favourite

Jab koi bhavvachak sangya (abstract noun) aam ya samanya roop se prayukt ho to article nahi lagta; par jab kisi vishisht vyakti ya ghatna se juda ho to 'the' lagana anivarya hai:

• General sense: Men fear death. / Courage is admired everywhere. (NO ARTICLE)
• Specific sense: The death of the Prime Minister left his party in shock.
• The courage of the wounded soldier was praised by all.

2. Possessive Case aur Possessive Adjectives ke baad

• the boy's uncle (= the uncle of the boy; yahan uncle se pehle 'the' nahi aayega!)
• It is my book. (= The book is mine; 'my the book' ya 'the my book' kehna galat hai).

3. Meals & Games/Sports ke aage

• Meals: The Scots have porridge for breakfast. / We had dinner together.
• Exception (Banquet/Formal feast): The wedding breakfast was held in her mother's house.
• Games & Sports: He plays golf. / She loves cricket. / We play chess. (Kabhi 'the golf' ya 'the cricket' mat bolo!)

4. Parts of the Body and Clothing — Special Contact Rule!

Important Contrast

Aamtaur par sharir ke angon aur kapdon ke aage possessive adjective (my, your, his, her) lagta hai:

• Raise your right hand. (NOT: the right hand ✗)
• He took off his coat. (NOT: the coat ✗)

Lekin Prepositional phrases of physical contact me 'the' ka prayog hota hai:

She seized the child by the collar. (Bachhe ko collar se pakda — 'by the collar')
I patted him on the shoulder. (Uski peeth/kandhe ko thapthapaya — 'on the shoulder')
The brick hit John in the face. (Eent John ke chehre par lagi — 'in the face')
He was hit on the head. (Passive me: Uske sir par chot lagi — 'on the head')

5. Plural Nouns in General Sense & "Nature"

• General plurals: Women are expected to like babies. (= women in general; agar 'the women' bolenge to kisi khaas group ki baat hogi).
• Big hotels all over the world are very much the same.
• Nature: Jab 'nature' ka matlab prakriti (spirit of plant & animal world) ho, to bina 'the' ke likhte hain:
  If you interfere with nature you will suffer for it.

Unit 03 · 8. Institutions & Places

8. Omission before home, school, hospital, work, etc.

Primary purpose (mukhya uddeshya) vs Other purpose (visitor visit) ka sabse prashidh niyam.

A. "Home" ke Rules

• Home akela ho (Used alone): 'the' nahi lagta:
  He is at home. / He went home. / I arrived home after dark.
• Home ke aage koi vivaran ya descriptive word ho: Normal noun ki tarah 'the' ya possessive lagta hai:
  They went to their new home. / We arrived at the bride's home.
  For some years this was the home of your queen. / A mud hut was the only home he had ever known.

B. Bed, Church, Court, Hospital, Prison, School/University

Must Master Table

In sthanon par agar aap wahan ke mukhya uddeshya (primary purpose) ke liye jayein to NO ARTICLE; lekin kisi doosre kaaran (visitor ban kar) jayein to 'the' anivarya hai:

⇄ Swipe table horizontally on mobile (मोबाइल पर पूरी टेबल देखने के लिए खिसकाएं)
Institution Primary Purpose (NO 'the' ✓) Secondary / Visitor Purpose ('THE' compulsory ✓)
School / College / University go to school (padhne ke liye as students)
leave school, at school		 I went to the school to meet the headmaster. (Principal se milne visitor ban kar)
Hospital go to hospital / in hospital (as patients - bimaar ho kar ilaaj karwane) I went to the hospital to visit my sick friend. (Dost ko dekhne)
Prison sent to prison / in prison (as prisoners - kaidi ban kar saza katne) He goes to the prison sometimes to give lectures. (Bhashan dene)
Church go to church / at church (as worshippers - prarthna karne) I went to the church to see the stained glass. (Sundar kaanch dekhne)
Bed go to bed / in bed (sone ya aaram karne ke liye) He sat on the bed to tie his shoelaces. (Joote baandhne baitha)
Court go to court / in court (litigants ya gawaah ban kar) The tourists visited the court building. (Building dekhne)

C, D & E. Sea, Work vs Office, and Town

Sea as Sailors vs Beach Holiday • We go to sea as sailors. / To be at sea = yatra par hona.
• To go to / be at the sea = samudra tat (seaside/beach) par jana.
Work vs Office vs In Office • work (= place of work): He's at work. (NO the)
• office (= workplace): He is in the office. (Needs 'the')
• Idiom: to be in office = rajneetik pad/power me hona.
Town (Apna Shehar) Speaker ke apne shehar ke liye 'the' chhod sakte hain:
• We go to town to buy clothes. / We were in town last Monday.

Unit 04 · 9. Demonstratives

9. this / these, that / those

Demonstrative adjectives aur pronouns ke prayog aur sanket (pointing) ke niyam.

A. Demonstrative Adjectives: Noun ke saath number me match karte hain

English me Demonstratives hi akele aise adjectives hain jo noun ke number (singular/plural) ke sath badalte hain:

• Near (Paas): This beach was empty. (Singular) / These people come from that hotel. (Plural)
• Far / Past (Door ya beeta samay): What does that notice say? (Singular) / Do you see those birds? (Plural)
• He was dismissed on the 13th. That night the factory caught fire.
✦ Emphatic Construction (Aksar asantosh ya shikayat jatane ke liye):
• This diet of mine isn't having much effect. (= My diet)
• That car of Ann's is always breaking down. (= Ann's car)

B. Demonstratives as Pronouns

• Basic Pronouns: This is my umbrella. That's yours.
• These are the old classrooms. Those are the new ones.
• Radio / Concert announcement: That was the concerto in C minor by Vivaldi.
• Introductions (Parichay karwate waqt): Ann: "Tom, this is my brother Hugh."
• Telephone calls: "Good morning. This is Tom Jones..." (Stranger ke liye 'I am Tom Jones' thoda formal hai, jabki 'Tom here' informal hai).
• Relative clauses me: Those who couldn't walk were carried on stretchers.
• Puri pichhli baat ya clause ko represent karne me:
  They're digging up my road. They do this every summer.
  He said I wasn't a good wife. Wasn't that a horrible thing to say?

C. Demonstratives with 'one / ones'

• Jab tulna (comparison) ya selection ho to 'one/ones' lagana aam hai par optional ho sakta hai:
  This chair is too low. I'll sit in that (one). / I like this (one) best.
• ⚠️ LEKIN jab demonstrative ke baad koi ADJECTIVE aa jaye, to 'one/ones' lagana ANIVARYA (essential) hai:
  I like this blue one. (NOT: I like this blue ✗)
  I like these blue ones. (NOT: I like these blue ✗)
