Unit 01 · Sound & Pronunciation

1. a / an (The Indefinite Article)

A ya An ka lagna English letters par nahi, balki bolte waqt nikalne wali pehli aawaz (sound) par depend karta hai.

Rule 1: Consonant Sound ke aage "a" aur Vowel Sound ke aage "an"

Basic Rule
👉 Simple Trick: Hindi ke swar (स्वर: अ, आ, इ, ई, उ, ऊ, ए, ऐ, ओ, औ) ki aawaz se shuru ho to "an" lagao. Agar vyanjan (व्यंजन: क, ख, ग, य, र, ल, व...) ki aawaz se shuru ho to "a" lagao!
a boy, a table, a cat Normal consonant sounds (/b/, /t/, /k/)
an apple, an egg, an umbrella Normal vowel sounds (/æ/, /e/, /ʌ/)

Rule 2: Exam Traps — Vowel letters par Consonant Sound

High Yield

Kuch words dekhne me E, U ya O se shuru hote hain, lekin unki bolne ki aawaz /j/ ("य") ya /w/ ("व") hoti hai. Isliye inke aage "a" lagta hai:

Word Pronunciation (उच्चारण) Correct Form Note
University "यू-नि-वर-सि-टी" (starts with /j/ 'य') a university ✓ (NOT an) Vyanjan 'य' sound hai, isliye 'a' lagta hai.
European "यू-रो-पि-अन" (starts with /j/ 'य') a European ✓ (NOT an) Shuruat 'E' se hai par sound 'य' hai.
One-way / One-rupee "वन-वे" (starts with /w/ 'व') a one-way road ✓ 'O' dekhkar an mat lagana, aawaz 'व' hai.
Uniform / Union / Unique "यू-नि-फॉर्म" (starts with /j/ 'य') a uniform, a unique idea ✓ Sabhi me 'य' ki aawaz aati hai.

Rule 3: Silent 'h' aur Abbreviations me "an" ka use

Must Remember
👉 Silent 'h': In words me 'h' bola nahi jata: an hour (आवर), an honest man (ऑनेस्ट), an heir (एयर = उत्तराधिकारी), an honour (ऑनर)।
👉 Abbreviations (Short forms): Jab letters M, N, F, S, X, L ko akele bolte hain to pehli aawaz vowel /e/ ('ए') nikalati hai:
an MP, an MLA, an MBA 'M' ko bolte waqt "एम" (vowel 'ए') nikalta hai.
an FIR, an SOS, an SDM, an SP 'F' bolte waqt "एफ़" aur 'S' bolte waqt "एस" nikalta hai.
an X-ray, an L-plate 'X' ("एक्स") aur 'L' ("एल") vowel sound se shuru hote hain.
Contrast: a VIP, a B.Ed student 'V' ("वी") aur 'B' ("बी") consonant sound hain, isliye 'a' lagta hai.

Unit 02 · Applications

2. Use of a / an

A aur An ka use kin-kin situations aur phrases me hota hai.

A. Pehli baar kisi Singular Countable Noun ko mention karne par

Jab kisi aam cheez ya person ka pehli baar zikr karein jise sunne wala pehle se nahi janta:

• I bought a pen and an umbrella yesterday.
• She lives in a flat in Delhi.

B. Pura Class / Group represent karne ke liye

Jab ek example dekar pure category ya class ki baat ho rahi ho:

• A car must be insured. (= Sabhi cars ka insurance hona zaroori hai)
• A child needs love and care. (= Har bachhe ko pyaar ki zaroorat hoti hai)

C. Professions, Jobs aur Titles ke saath

Kisi bhi vyakti ka profession (pesha ya kaam) batane ke liye a/an lagana compulsory hai:

• My mother is a teacher. / He is an engineer.
• She wants to be a doctor. / He is an Indian.

D. Rate, Speed, Price aur Ratio (Jahan a/an ka matlab "per" ho)

Bhav (price), speed ya frequency batane me 'a/an' ka matlab hota hai 'prati' (per):

• Apples are ₹100 a kilo. (= ₹100 per kilo)
• The train runs at 80 km an hour. (= 80 km per hour)
• Take this medicine twice a day. (= twice per day)

E. Exclamations me: "What a..." aur "Such a..."

Singular countable noun ke saath a/an lagta hai, par plural ya uncountable me NAHI lagta:

• What a pretty girl! / Such a pleasant surprise! (Singular countable: 'a' aayega)
• What lovely flowers! (Plural: koi article nahi aayega!)
• What terrible weather! (Uncountable: koi article nahi aayega!)

F. Anjaan vyakti: "a Mr Smith" / "a Mrs Sharma"

Jab bolne wala kisi vyakti ko personal level par nahi janta ho:

• A Mr Sharma called you. (= Sharma naam ke kisi anjaan vyakti ka phone aaya tha)
• Agar 'Mr Sharma' bina 'a' ke likhein to iska matlab speaker unhe pehle se janta hai.

G. Numbers, Fractions aur "Half" ke rules

• ½ kg = half a kilo (Dhayan dein: 'half' se pehle 'a' nahi lagta!)
• 1½ kg = one and a half kilos ('and' ke baad 'a' aayega)
• Expressions of quantity: a lot of, a couple of, a dozen, a great many.

Unit 03 · Omission

3. Omission of a / an

Kahan a/an lagana bilkul mana (prohibited) hai — sabse common exam mistakes yahin hoti hain!

1. Plural Nouns ke aage a/an kabhi nahi lagta

A/An ka matlab hota hai 'ek' (singular). Isliye plural nouns ke aage yeh nahi lagta:

• a dog → dogs (NOT a dogs ✗)
• an egg → eggs (NOT an eggs ✗)

2. Uncountable Nouns ke aage a/an kabhi nahi lagta

Sabse Bada Exam Trap

Uncountable nouns (jinko 1, 2, 3 gin nahi sakte) ke aage direct 'a' ya 'an' lagana galat hai:

Noun Galat (Incorrect) Sahi (Correct English) Kaise bole / Hindi
Advice (सलाह) an advice ✗, advices ✗ advice / a piece of advice ✓ 'A piece of advice' bolo agar ek salah kehna ho.
Bread (रोटी/ब्रेड) a bread ✗, breads ✗ bread / a loaf of bread ✓ 'A loaf of bread' ya 'a slice of bread' bolo.
Furniture (सामान) a furniture ✗, furnitures ✗ furniture / an item of furniture ✓ Furniture hamesha uncountable rehta hai.
Luggage / Baggage a luggage ✗, luggages ✗ luggage / a piece of luggage ✓ Samman ko count karne ke liye 'piece of luggage' use karo.
News (समाचार) a news ✗ news / a piece of news ✓ News dekhne me plural lagta hai par hota uncountable hai.
Information an information ✗ information / some information ✓ An information bolna grammatically galat hai.

3. Daily Meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) ke rules

• Normal daily meals: Koi article nahi lagta — We have breakfast at 8:00 AM. / Come for dinner.
• EXCEPTION 1 (Adjective aane par): Agar meal ke aage koi adjective aa jaye to 'a' lagta hai — We had a delicious breakfast. / He served a heavy lunch.
• EXCEPTION 2 (Formal Banquet / Party): Kisi ke samman me di gayi party/banquet ke liye: They hosted a dinner to welcome the guests.

Unit 04 · Comparisons

4. a / an and "one"

A/An aur One dono ka matlab 'ek' hota hai, lekin dono ke use me bohot subtle farq hai.

1. Type (प्रकार) vs Number (संख्यात्मक गिनती)

• "A shotgun is no good." → Yahan weapon ke TYPE (प्रकार) ki baat ho rahi hai ki shotgun galat weapon hai, rifle chahiye.
• "One shotgun is no good." → Yahan NUMBER (संख्या) ki baat ho rahi hai ki ek shotgun kaafi nahi hai, humein 2 ya 3 shotguns chahiye!

2. Rates aur Ratios me "a" ko "one" se replace nahi kar sakte

Currency me aap dono bol sakte ho: £1 = a pound ya one pound. 1,000 = a thousand ya one thousand.

⚠️ Lekin Rates aur Speed me sirf 'a/an' aayega:
• The rent is £150 a week. (NOT: £150 one week ✗)
• Sixty kilometres an hour. (NOT: sixty kilometres one hour ✗)

3. "One" ke special use-cases

• One... another: One boy wanted to study, another wanted to play.
• Past specific event: One winter night, there was a terrible storm.
• Future date: One day you will understand.
• Pronoun replacement: "Did you get a ticket?" → "Yes, I got one." (Iska plural some hota hai: "Yes, I got some.")
