Unit 01 · Classification & Functional Roles

1. Kinds and Classification of Adverbs (Section 28)

A. The Functional Classes of Adverbs (क्रियाविशेषण के 8 प्रमुख प्रकार)

Core Categories
👉 सरल समझ (Simple Hindi Explanation):
क्रियाविशेषण (Adverb) वह शब्द है जो किसी क्रिया (verb), विशेषण (adjective) या अन्य क्रियाविशेषण की विशेषता, ढंग, समय, स्थान, आवृत्ति या तीव्रता बताता है:
1. Manner (रीतिवाचक - 'How?'): bravely, fast, happily, hard, quickly, well.
2. Place (स्थानवाचक - 'Where?'): by, down, here, near, there, up.
3. Time (कालवाचक - 'When?'): now, soon, still, then, today, yet.
4. Frequency (आवृत्तिवाचक - 'How often?'): always, never, occasionally, often, twice.
5. Interrogative & Relative (प्रश्नवाचक व संबंधवाचक): when, where, why.
Adverb Class Core Question Examples Audio Pronunciation 🔊
Manner (रीतिवाचक) How? (कैसे?) bravely, fast, quickly, well
Place (स्थानवाचक) Where? (कहाँ?) here, there, down, near, up
Time (कालवाचक) When? (कब?) now, soon, still, then, today, yet
Frequency (आवृत्ति) How often? (कितनी बार?) always, never, often, twice

B. Sentence Adverbs vs Adverbs of Degree (वाक्य क्रियाविशेषण व परिमाणवाचक)

Special Function
👉 Sentence Adverbs (वाक्य क्रियाविशेषण):
ये केवल किसी एक क्रिया की विशेषता नहीं बताते, बल्कि संपूर्ण वाक्य या कथन पर वक्ता की टिप्पणी (speaker's opinion/judgment) व्यक्त करते हैं:
• certainly, definitely, luckily, surely
• Luckily, no one was injured in the collision. (सौभाग्य से, किसी को चोट नहीं आई।)
• He will certainly agree to our proposal. (वह निश्चित रूप से सहमत होगा।)

👉 Degree Adverbs (परिमाण/तीव्रतावाचक - 'How much / To what extent?'):
ये किसी विशेषण या क्रियाविशेषण की तीव्रता (intensity) मापते हैं:
• fairly, hardly, rather, quite, too, very
• The exam was quite difficult. / He was too tired to move.
⚠️ परीक्षा चेतावनी (Sentence Adverb vs Manner Trap): He spoke surely (manner) का प्रयोग नहीं होता; Surely he will come (sentence adverb = वक्ता का विश्वास) मानक है। इसी तरह luckily पूरे वाक्य को संशोधित करता है: Luckily, the train was late (not 'The train arrived luckily' ✗)।
Unit 02 · Morphological Formation & Spelling

2. Formation of Adverbs with -ly & Spelling Rules (Section 29 A)

A. Suffixation with -ly & Consonant/Vowel Transformations

Spelling Mechanics
👉 अधिकांश रीतिवाचक (Manner) व कुछ परिमाणवाचक क्रियाविशेषण विशेषण में -ly जोड़कर बनते हैं:
• grave → gravely, immediate → immediately, slow → slowly.

👉 चार अनिवार्य वर्तनी नियम (4 Essential Spelling Rules):
• (a) अंतिम 'y' को 'i' में बदलना: यदि विशेषण व्यंजन + y पर समाप्त हो, तो y हटाकर -ily लगाते हैं:
  happy → happily, heavy → heavily, easy → easily, busy → busily.
• (c) -able / -ible में अंतिम 'e' हटाकर केवल 'y' लगाना:
  capable → capably, sensible → sensibly, probable → probably, terrible → terribly.
• (d) स्वर + l (Vowel + l) के बाद सामान्य नियम: '-ly' जुड़ता है, जिससे डबल 'll' बन जाता है:
  beautiful → beautifully, final → finally, hopeful → hopefully, cool → coolly.
Adjective Spelling Rule Applied Adverb Form Audio 🔊
happy Final y changes to i happily
capable Drop final e and add y capably
sensible Drop final e and add y sensibly
beautiful Vowel + l adds -ly (double l) beautifully

B. Retention of Final 'e' & The 3 Critical Exceptions (true, due, whole)

Top Exam Trap
👉 सामान्य नियम: अंतिम 'e' बना रहता है (A final 'e' is retained):
• extreme → extremely
• brave → bravely
• complete → completely
• safe → safely
• polite → politely
⚠️ परीक्षा के 3 स्वर्णिम अपवाद (Exceptions that drop 'e'): प्रतियोगी परीक्षाओं में सबसे ज्यादा पूछे जाने वाले तीन अपवाद:
1. true → truly (कदापि 'truely' न लिखें ✗)
2. due → duly (कदापि 'duely' न लिखें ✗)
3. whole → wholly (कदापि 'wholely' न लिखें ✗)
उदाहरण: I am truly grateful. / The forms were duly signed. / I am wholly in agreement.
Unit 03 · Syntactic Traps & Semantic Shifts

3. The '-ly Adjectives' Trap & Divergent Meanings (Section 29 B & C)

A. Good vs Well & The '-ly Adjectives' Trap (friendly, lonely, silly)

Deceptive Forms
👉 1. Good का क्रियाविशेषण 'Well' है:
• He is a good driver (adjective) → He drives well (adverb). (कदापि 'goodly' या क्रिया के बाद 'good' न कहें)।

👉 2. kindly का दोहरा रूप:
• kindly विशेषण और क्रियाविशेषण दोनों हो सकता है: a kindly smile (adj) / He kindly helped us (adv).

👉 3. The Fatal Trap — Adjectives ending in -ly:
• friendly, likely, lonely, lovely, silly, ugly, cowardly, brotherly — ये सभी केवल विशेषण (adjectives) हैं! इनका कोई क्रियाविशेषण रूप नहीं होता!
• अतः हम कभी नहीं कह सकते: He spoke friendly ✗ या She smiled lovely ✗.
• समाधान (Prepositional Adverb Phrase): in a ... way / manner का प्रयोग करें:
  ✓ He spoke in a friendly way.
  ✓ She greeted us in a lovely manner.
  ✓ He acted in a cowardly manner.

B. Adverbs with Divergent or Narrower Meanings (Feelings vs Physical State)

Semantic Precision
👉 भावनाओं (Feelings) के लिए प्रयुक्त क्रियाविशेषण:
कुछ क्रियाविशेषण अपने मूल विशेषण की तुलना में संकुचित या भिन्न अर्थ ग्रहण कर लेते हैं:
• coldly: We received them coldly (= रूखेपन से, अनमने ढंग से, in an unfriendly way).
• hotly: They denied the accusation hotly (= क्रोधपूर्वक/रोष से, indignantly).
• warmly: She welcomed us warmly (= आत्मीयता से, in a cordial/friendly way).
  ⚠️ लेकिन: warmly dressed = गर्म कपड़े पहने हुए (wearing warm clothes).
• coolly: शांत व साहसपूर्वक (calmly/courageously) या धृष्टता से (impudently):
  He behaved very coolly in this dangerous situation. (संकट में भी शांत रहा।)
• presently: इसका अर्थ 'soon' (शीघ्र ही) होता है (वर्तमान में नहीं!):
  He will be here presently. (= He will be here soon.)
Unit 04 · Identical Forms & Semantic Splits

4. Same Form Adverbs/Adjectives & The -ly Dual Meaning Pairs (Section 30)

A. Words Having the Same Form as Adjectives and Adverbs

Identical Forms
👉 अनेक शब्द बिना कोई -ly जोड़े ही क्रियाविशेषण और विशेषण दोनों का कार्य करते हैं:
back, deep, direct, early, enough, far, fast, hard, high, ill, just, kindly, late, left, little, long, low, much, near, pretty, right, short, still, straight, well, wrong.

ध्यान दें कि वाक्य में स्थिति (position) से इनका भेद स्पष्ट होता है:
Word Used as Adverb (क्रियाविशेषण) Used as Adjective (विशेषण) Audio 🔊
fast The train went fast. (क्रिया went को संशोधित करता है) a fast train (संज्ञा train को संशोधित करता है)
hard They worked hard. (= energetically) The work is hard. (= difficult)
direct You can dial Rome direct. (= directly, straight) the most direct route
straight She went straight home. a straight line
ill an ill-made road (badly made) You look ill. (sick)

B. The Famous -ly Dual Meaning Pairs (hard/hardly, late/lately, high/highly)

Crucial Examination Pairs
👉 समान मूल वाले शब्दों के दो क्रियाविशेषण रूप और उनके बिल्कुल भिन्न अर्थ:
1. hard vs hardly:
  • hard: परिश्रम से, जोर से — He works hard.
  • hardly: मुश्किल से / शायद ही / ना के बराबर — He hardly works. (वह काम ही नहीं करता!)
2. late vs lately:
  • late: देर से — He arrived late.
  • lately: हाल ही में (recently) — Have you seen him lately?
3. high vs highly:
  • high: भौतिक ऊँचाई — The bird flew high.
  • highly: अमूर्त अर्थ में (abstractly/greatly) — He was a highly paid official. / They spoke very highly of him.
4. deep vs deeply:
  • deep: भौतिक गहराई — He dug deep into the soil.
  • deeply: आंतरिक भावनाओं के लिए — He was deeply offended.
5. near vs nearly:
  • near: निकट/समीप — Come near.
  • nearly: लगभग (almost) — I am nearly ready.
6. pretty vs prettily:
  • pretty: तीव्रतावाचक (very/quite) — The exam was pretty difficult.
  • prettily: आकर्षक ढंग से (attractively) — Her little girls are prettily dressed.
7. shortly: जल्द ही / संक्षेप में (soon, briefly or curtly) — The train arrives shortly.
8. rightly vs wrongly: Past participle के साथ न्यायसंगत/त्रुटिपूर्ण रूप से — He was rightly punished. / You were wrongly informed.
Unit 05 · Usage Constraints & Comparison Degrees

5. Restrictions on Long/Near & Comparison of Adverbs (Sections 30 C & 31)

A. Restricted Uses of 'Long' and 'Near' (सकारात्मक वाक्यों में सीमाएँ)

Syntactic Limits
👉 1. 'long' (क्रियाविशेषण) का नियम:
• longer, longest बिना किसी प्रतिबंध के हर जगह आ सकते हैं: It took longer than I expected.
• लेकिन positive रूप 'long' केवल नकारात्मक या प्रश्नवाचक वाक्यों में आता है:
  • How long will it take to get there? (प्रश्नवाचक) ✓
  • It won't take long. (नकारात्मक) ✓
• ⚠️ सकारात्मक (Affirmative) में: हम अकेला 'long' नहीं कह सकते (It will take long ✗)!
  इसके स्थान पर too long, so long, long enough अथवा a long time कहा जाता है:
  ✓ It will take a long time. / It would take too long.
  बोलचाल में 'for a long time' की जगह अक्सर (for) ages आता है: I waited for ages.

👉 2. 'near' (क्रियाविशेषण) का नियम:
• nearer, nearest स्वतंत्र रूप से प्रयुक्त होते हैं: Don't come any nearer.
• लेकिन positive रूप 'near' साधारणतया very, quite, so, too या enough के साथ आता है:
  ✓ They live quite near. / Don't come too near. / You're near enough.
  संबोधक रूप (Preposition near + noun): Don't go near the edge.

B. Comparative and Superlative Adverb Forms (क्रियाविशेषण की तुलना)

Comparative Morphology
👉 1. दो या दो से अधिक शब्दांशों (Two or more syllables) वाले क्रियाविशेषण:
इनके पहले more और most लगाया जाता है:
• quickly → more quickly → most quickly
• fortunately → more fortunately → most fortunately
• carefully → more carefully → most carefully

👉 2. एक शब्दांश (Single-syllable) वाले क्रियाविशेषण और 'early':
इनमें -er और -est जोड़ते हैं:
• hard → harder → hardest
• fast → faster → fastest
• late → later → latest
• early → earlier → earliest (अंतिम 'y' का 'i' बनता है)

👉 3. अनियमित तुलना (Irregular Comparisons):
• well → better → best
• badly → worse → worst
• little → less → least
• much → more → most
• far → farther → farthest (केवल भौतिक दूरी के लिए)
• far → further → furthest (भौतिक दूरी तथा अतिरिक्त/विस्तृत दोनों के लिए)
