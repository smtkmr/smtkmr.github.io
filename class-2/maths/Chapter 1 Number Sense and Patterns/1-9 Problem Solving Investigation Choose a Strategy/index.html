Three useful strategies

Choose how to get started

More than one method can work. Pick one that helps you see the problem.

🖍️

Draw a picture

Sketch objects, groups, shapes or a simple diagram. Pictures help you count, compare and see what is missing.

● ● ●+● ●=5
🧠

Use logical reasoning

Use every clue. Decide what must be true and cross out ideas that do not fit.

not first→check clues
🎭

Act it out

Use counters, move your body, or pretend to be in the story. Acting helps you follow each step in order.

start→move→count
