Compare Numbers

Find which number is greater, which is less, or whether two numbers are equal. Compare the tens first, then the ones if the tens are the same.

Learn the comparison signs

Which number is greater?

Compare one place at a time.

>

Greater than

> means the number on the left is greater. Example: 74 > 49.

<

Less than

< means the number on the left is less. Example: 21 < 22.

=

Equal to

= means both numbers have the same value. Example: 78 = 78.

>

Greater than

Big number first: 86 > 53

<

Less than

Small number first: 35 < 56

=

Equal to

Same number: 83 = 83

Step 1 · Compare tens

Compare 64 and 46

64 has 6 tens. 46 has 4 tens. Six tens is greater, so 64 > 46.

64>46

Step 2 · If tens match, compare ones

Compare 53 and 58

Both have 5 tens. Compare ones: 3 is less than 8, so 53 < 58.

53<58
1

Look at the tens

More tens means a greater number. For example, 8 tens is more than 5 tens.

2

Check the ones

If the tens digits match, compare the ones digits to decide.

3

Choose the sign

Say the comparison aloud: greater, less, or equal. Then write >, <, or =.

Tip: Read the sign from left to right. In 74 > 49, 74 is greater than 49.
