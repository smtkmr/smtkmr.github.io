Part 1 · Learn the idea

Numbers are made of groups

We can bundle 10 ones together to make 1 ten. Then we count the tens and the ones left over.

🔟 A ten is a bundle

When we have 10 ones, we can bundle them into 1 ten. Ten ones and one ten have the same value.

10 ones=
1 ten

🧩 The digits have places

A two-digit number has a tens place and a ones place. The digit’s place tells its value.

TensGroups of ten
OnesSingle units
ValueHow much a digit is worth

Let’s unpack 34

In 34, the 3 is in the tens place. It means 3 tens, or 30. The 4 is in the ones place. It means 4 ones, or 4.

Part 3 · Think like a mathematician

How to read a two-digit number

1

Look at the tens digit

In 62, the 6 is in the tens place. It stands for 6 groups of ten: 60.

2

Look at the ones digit

The 2 is in the ones place. It stands for 2 single ones.

3

Put the parts together

60 + 2 = 62. So 62 is 6 tens and 2 ones.

Watch out! 26 and 62 use the same digits, but the digits are in different places. 26 is 2 tens and 6 ones. 62 is 6 tens and 2 ones.
