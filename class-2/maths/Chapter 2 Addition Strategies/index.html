Worksheet Summary

Tens & Ones Rules!
• 1. Grouping Rule: 10 Ones = 1 Ten (10 इकाइयाँ = 1 दहाई)।
• 2. Expanded Form: 57 = 5 tens + 7 ones = 50 + 7 = 57!
• 3. Place Value of Digit: In 73, digit 7 is in tens place = 70. In 54, digit 4 is in ones place = 4.
• 4. Comparing Rule: 43 books vs 34 students: 4 tens > 3 tens, so 43 > 34 (Enough books!).

Worksheet Sheet 2 · Addition Sentences

2. Write an Addition Sentence to Solve (जोड़ के वाक्य)

A. Visual Groups: Pea Pods (मटर) & Apple Bags (सेब)

Sheet 2: Problems 1 & 2
Problem 1: How many peas? (मटर)

चित्र में 3 फली (pods) हैं, प्रत्येक में 10 मटर के दाने हैं, और 4 दाने बाहर अलग रखे हैं:

3 pods of 10 = 30 + 4 loose peas = 4
30 + 4 = 34
कुल 34 मटर (3 tens + 4 ones = 34)
Problem 2: How many apples? (सेब)

चित्र में सेब के 2 बैग हैं (प्रत्येक बैग में 10 सेब = 20), और 5 सेब खुले रखे हैं:

2 bags of 10 = 20 + 5 loose apples = 5
20 + 5 = 25
कुल 25 सेब (2 tens + 5 ones = 25)

B. Real-World Place Value Comparisons from Sheet 3

Sheet 3: Problems 5 & 6
Problem 5: Bookcase & Students

"A bookcase has 43 books. There are 34 students in the class. Are there enough books for the students? How do you know?"

• 43 has 4 tens (40).
• 34 has 3 tens (30).
• चूँकि 4 tens > 3 tens, इसलिए 43 > 34!
उत्तर: Yes! There are enough books because 43 > 34.
Problem 6: Concert Children & Boxes

"There are 75 children in the concert. There are 8 boxes of song books. There are 10 books in each box. Is there a book for each child in the concert? How do you know?"

• 8 boxes of 10 books = 8 tens = 80 books.
• बच्चे केवल 75 हैं।
• चूँकि 80 > 75, इसलिए हर बच्चे को किताब मिलेगी!
उत्तर: Yes! 80 books is more than 75 children.
