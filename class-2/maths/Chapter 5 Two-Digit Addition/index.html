Chapter 5 Golden Rules

Addition Rules & Traps!
• 1. Add Tens Fact: 7 tens + 2 tens = 9 tens ➔ 70 + 20 = 90.
• 2. Count On: 47 + 40 ➔ count on 4 tens from 47: 57, 67, 77, 87.
• 3. Work Backward: If Uncle Joe brings 12 more than Aunt Patty, and Patty brings 5 more than Grandpa (10): start at Grandpa (10) ➔ Patty (15) ➔ Joe (27)!
• 4. Regrouping Rule: If ones sum is 10 or greater (e.g. 24 + 7: 4+7 = 11), regroup 10 ones as 1 ten!
• 5. Tough Multi-Step: Read carefully! Lydia collects 20 red and twice as many black pencils = 20 + 40 = 60 pencils in all!

⭐ Tough Problem 1: Lydia's Red & Black Pencils

"Lydia collects 20 red pencils. She has twice as many black pencils as red pencils. How many pencils does Lydia have in all?"

• Red pencils = 20
• Black pencils = twice of 20 = 20 + 20 = 40
• Total pencils in all = 20 + 40 = 60 pencils!
⚠️ Common Trap (सावधानी): कई बच्चे केवल 40 लिख देते हैं। प्रश्न में "in all" पूछा है, इसलिए लाल और काली दोनों को जोड़ना होगा!
⭐ Tough Problem 2: Steve & Sal's Paper Airplanes

"Steve has thirty paper airplanes. His friend Sal has ten fewer airplanes. How many paper airplanes do the two friends have?"

• Steve = 30 airplanes
• Sal = 10 fewer than 30 = 30 - 10 = 20 airplanes
• Together = 30 + 20 = 50 airplanes!

4. Add Two-Digit Numbers & Multi-Step Decision Problems

A. Sara's Apples (Two Pies?) & Sandy's Pen (87 Cents?)

Lesson 5-6 Problem-Solving
Worksheet 5-6, Prob 5: Sara's Apples

"Sara picks 24 apples. Her brother picks 33 apples. How many apples do they pick altogether? Their mom needs 21 apples to bake a pie. Can she bake two pies? Explain."

• Total picked = 24 + 33 = 57 apples.
• Apples needed for 2 pies = 21 + 21 = 42 apples.
• Can mom bake two pies? YES! Because 57 apples is greater than 42 apples (57 > 42).
Worksheet 5-6, Prob 6: Sandy's Pen

"Sandy wants to buy a pen. It costs 87 cents. She has 7 dimes and 26 pennies. Can she buy the pen? Prove your answer."

• 7 dimes = 7 tens = 70 cents.
• 26 pennies = 26 cents.
• Total money Sandy has = 70 + 26 = 96 cents.
• Can she buy the pen? YES! Because 96 cents > 87 cents (she has 9 cents left over).
