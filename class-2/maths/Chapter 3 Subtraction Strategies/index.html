1 Count Back

Count Back 1, 2, or 3

Start at the bigger number and jump backward by 1, 2, or 3 on the number line.

9 − 3 = ?
Start at 9: 8 (hop 1), 7 (hop 2), 6 (hop 3)!
2 Zero & All

Subtract All & Subtract Zero

When you subtract all, nothing is left (0). When you subtract 0, the number never changes!

8 − 8 = 0 (Subtract All)
8 − 0 = 8 (Subtract Zero)
3 Doubles

Use Doubles to Subtract

If you know your addition doubles, subtraction is instant! 7 + 7 = 14, so 14 − 7 = 7.

12 − 6 = 6
Because 6 + 6 = 12!
4 Patterns

Find a Pattern in Subtraction

Spotting number relationships makes subtraction rapid and helps solve complex worksheets.

10 − 1 = 9  |  10 − 2 = 8
10 − 3 = 7  |  10 − 4 = 6
5 Inverse Ops

Relate Addition & Subtraction

Addition puts parts together; subtraction takes one part away from the whole!

5 + 3 = 8 ⟷ 8 − 3 = 5
Part 1 + Part 2 = Whole
6 & 7 Fact Families

Missing Addends & Fact Families

Every family of 3 numbers creates 2 addition and 2 subtraction facts living under one roof!

4 + 7 = 11  |  7 + 4 = 11
11 − 4 = 7  |  11 − 7 = 4
