Take a Survey & Tally Charts

सर्वेक्षण करना और टैली चिह्नों से डेटा दर्ज करना

What is a Survey? (सर्वेक्षण क्या है?)

A survey is a way of collecting information or data by asking people a specific question (e.g., "What is your favorite game?" or "What breakfast food do you like best?").

Rule for Tally Marks:
• Mark 1 to 4 with vertical sticks: | = 1, || = 2, ||| = 3, |||| = 4.
• For the 5th mark, draw a diagonal slash across the four sticks to make a closed bundle of 5: |||| = 5.
• To count quickly, skip-count by 5s: 5, 10, 15... then add loose sticks!

Picture Graphs (Pictographs with Keys)

चित्र आलेख और प्रतीक कुंजी (Key: 1 प्रतीक = 2 वोट)

A Picture Graph uses symbols or pictures to represent data. Always check the KEY (कुंजी) at the bottom of the graph!

Key Rule: If the key states: Each 🌸 stands for 2 votes, then:
• 1 flower = 2 votes
• 2 flowers = 2 × 2 = 4 votes
• 3 flowers = 3 × 2 = 6 votes
• 5 flowers = 5 × 2 = 10 votes!

Strategy: Write a Number Sentence

संख्या वाक्य लिखना: जोड़ और घटाव (Addition & Subtraction Sentences)

When solving word problems with graphs, we write a math sentence:

• Comparison ("How many more... than..."): Use Subtraction: Larger - Smaller = Difference.
• Combining ("in all" or "either... or..."): Use Addition: Group 1 + Group 2 = Total.

Worksheet 4-3 Example: Mr. Bunn's Class Lunch
Mr. Bunn's Class Lunch

1. How many more students ate PB&J than salads? 12 - 4 = 8.
2. How many students ate either a salad or a tuna sandwich? 4 + 6 = 10.
3. How many students ate a sandwich? 12 + 6 = 18 (PB&J + Tuna).
4. If Mr. Bunn brings 6 extra salads, how many salads then? 4 + 6 = 10.

Line Plots, Mode and Range

लाइन प्लॉट, बहुलक (Mode) और परिसर (Range)
Golden Rule from Worksheet 4-6:
"Remember: MODE is the MOST; RANGE is the DIFFERENCE."
• Mode (बहुलक): The number that has the MOST 'X' marks (highest frequency).
• Range (परिसर): Greatest Number - Least Number.
