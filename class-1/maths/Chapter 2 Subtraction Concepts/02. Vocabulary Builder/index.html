Your review words

Four words. Two maths jobs.

Read each word. Addition puts groups together. Subtraction takes some away or splits a group.

Addition · groups grow

add to

Join some more to a group. Now there are more objects.

Addition · groups join

put together

Join two groups to find how many there are in all.

Subtraction · take away

take from

Start with a group. Some go away. Count how many are left.

Subtraction · split a group

take apart

Split one group into smaller parts. The whole group becomes parts.

Question 01Your turn

Sort “add to”

When we add to a group, we join more objects. The group gets bigger.

Short solution

“Add to” means join more to a group. The group grows, so it is addition.

Question 02Your turn

Sort “take from”

When we take from a group, some objects go away. The group gets smaller.

Short solution

“Take from” means remove some objects from a group. That is subtraction.

Question 03Your turn

Sort “put together”

When we put together, we join two groups to find how many there are in all.

Short solution

Two groups are joined to find the total. “Put together” belongs with addition.

Question 04Your turn

Sort “take apart”

When we take apart, we split one group into smaller parts.

Short solution

“Take apart” means split a whole group into parts. That is subtraction.

Question 05Your turn

Oranges are eaten

There are 5 oranges. 2 are eaten. Which words tell what happens?

🍊🍊🍊🍊🍊

Short solution

Two oranges are eaten, so they go away from the group. We take 2 from 5: “take from.”

Question 06Your turn

Balloons fly away

4 balloons are in the sky. 3 fly away. Is the group put together or taken from?

🎈🎈🎈🎈

Short solution

Three balloons fly away, so objects are taken from the group. This is subtraction.

Question 09Your turn

Take some cookies

A plate has 6 cookies. 2 are taken for a snack. Finish the sentence.

🍪🍪🍪🍪🍪🍪

We _____ 2 cookies from the group.

Short solution

Two cookies leave the group of 6. We “take from” the group.
