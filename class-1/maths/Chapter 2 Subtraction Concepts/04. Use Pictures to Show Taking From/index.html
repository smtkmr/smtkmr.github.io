Activity 01 · Listen and draw

Make a picture. Take some away.

Build the starting group with “Add one”. Tap each picture you want to take away. Count the ones that are not crossed out.

Question 01Your turn

Children in the sandbox

Draw 5 children in the sandbox. Select 2 who walk away, then tap “Take away selected”. How many children remain?

0 children · 0 selected · 0 taken away · 0 remain

Short solution

Draw 5 children. Cross out the 2 who walk away. Count the 3 still in the sandbox: 5 − 2 = 3.

Question 02Your turn

Birds on a branch

Draw 6 birds. Select 3 that fly away, then tap “Take away selected”. How many stay on the branch?

0 birds · 0 selected · 0 taken away · 0 remain

Short solution

Start with 6 birds. Cross out 3 that fly away. Three birds remain: 6 − 3 = 3.

Question 03Your turn

Ducks in the pond

Draw 8 ducks. Select 4 that swim away, then tap “Take away selected”. How many ducks remain?

0 ducks · 0 selected · 0 taken away · 0 remain

Short solution

Draw 8 ducks and cross out 4. Count the 4 ducks left: 8 − 4 = 4.

Activity 02 · Look and take from

What is left in the picture?

Count all the objects first. The crossed-out objects are taken away. Count the ones still showing.

Question 04Your turn

Oranges for snack

There are 5 oranges. 2 are eaten. How many oranges are there now?

🍊🍊🍊🍊🍊

Short solution

Start with 5 oranges. Take away 2 eaten oranges. Three oranges remain: 5 − 2 = 3.

Question 05Your turn

Balloons in the sky

4 balloons are floating. 3 fly away. How many balloons remain?

🎈🎈🎈🎈

Short solution

There were 4 balloons. Three fly away, so 4 − 3 = 1 balloon remains.

Question 06Your turn

Toys in the box

There are 7 toy cars. 4 roll out. How many stay in the box?

🚗🚗🚗🚗🚗🚗🚗

Short solution

Seven cars were in the box. Four rolled away. Count the 3 still there: 7 − 4 = 3.

Question 07Your turn

Count back to find what remains

Start at 8. Count back 5 numbers. Where do you land?

0 — 1 — 2 — 3 — 4 — 5 — 6 — 7 — 8

Short solution

Count back 5 steps from 8: 7, 6, 5, 4, 3. You land on 3.

Question 08Your turn

Sand toys at the beach

5 children have sand toys. 2 children take their toys home. How many children still have toys?

🧒🧒🧒🧒🧒

Short solution

Begin with 5 children. 2 go home, so 5 − 2 = 3 children still have toys.

Question 09Your turn

Apples for the picnic

6 apples are in a basket. 4 are eaten at the picnic. How many apples are left?

🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎

Short solution

Six apples start in the basket. Take away 4 eaten apples: 6 − 4 = 2 apples left.

Question 10Your turn

Explain with a number sentence

8 shells are on the sand. 3 wash back into the sea. Which number sentence shows how many stay?

🐚🐚🐚🐚🐚🐚🐚🐚

Short solution

Three shells wash away from the group of 8. Use subtraction: 8 − 3 = 5 shells stay.
