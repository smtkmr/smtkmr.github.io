Activity 01 · Make & take away

Cross some out. Count what’s left.

Make the number of dots in the question. Cross out the ones being taken away. Count the dots that remain.

Question 01Your turn

Show 5 take away 2

Make 5 red dots, then cross out 2.

0 dots · 0 crossed out

Short solution

Start with 5 dots. Cross out 2. Count the 3 dots left: 5 − 2 = 3.

Question 02Your turn

Show 6 take away 3

Make 6 red dots, then cross out 3.

0 dots · 0 crossed out

Short solution

Make 6 dots and cross out 3. Three dots remain: 6 − 3 = 3.

Question 03Your turn

Show 8 take away 5

Make 8 red dots, then cross out 5.

0 dots · 0 crossed out

Short solution

Start with 8. Cross out 5 and count what remains: 1, 2, 3. So 8 − 5 = 3.

Question 04Your turn

Apples on the table

🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎

Short solution

There were 5 apples. Take away 2: 5 − 2 = 3 apples left.

Question 05Your turn

Butterflies still here

🦋🦋🦋🦋

Short solution

Two of the 4 butterflies fly away. Count the 2 still here: 4 − 2 = 2.

Question 08Your turn

Fish behind the rock

5 fish are swimming. 2 hide behind a rock. How many can you still see?

🐟🐟🪨🐟🐟🐟

Short solution

Two fish hide, so 3 of the 5 are still visible: 5 − 2 = 3.

Question 09Your turn

Find the missing number

Think: how many do we take away to go from 8 to 3?

8 → 7 → 6 → 5 → 4 → 3

8 − ___ = 3

Short solution

Count back from 8 to 3: 7, 6, 5, 4, 3. That is 5 steps, so 8 − 5 = 3.

Question 10Your turn

All the cookies are gone

There are 4 cookies. All 4 are eaten. How many are left?

🍪🍪🍪🍪

Short solution

All 4 cookies are taken away. None are left: 4 − 4 = 0.
