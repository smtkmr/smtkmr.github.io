Our learning steps

What does “take from” mean?

Start with a whole group. Take some objects away. Count the ones that remain (are still there).

4−1=3

4 is the whole group · 1 is taken away · 3 remain

Try it together

Model and draw: cats

1 Draw 4 cats (or use 4 little objects).

2 Move 1 cat away. You can cross it out.

3 Count the cats still here: 1, 2, 3.

So, 4 − 1 = 3. Three cats remain!
