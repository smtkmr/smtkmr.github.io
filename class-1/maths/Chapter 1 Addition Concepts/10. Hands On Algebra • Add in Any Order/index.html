Your little word bank

Meet the ideas behind addition.

Addend
A number that is added to another number. In 1 + 3 = 4, the addends are 1 and 3.
Sum
The answer to an addition problem. In 1 + 3 = 4, the sum is 4.
Commutative property
Change the order of the addends, and the sum stays the same. 1 + 3 and 3 + 1 both make 4.

Look at our cube trains!

Switch the red and blue groups around. We haven’t added or removed any cubes, so there are still 4. That is why changing the order keeps the sum the same.

Math talk: Why is the sum the same across and down?

“The numbers being added are the same, so the total doesn’t change when written across or down.” Only the way we write the problem has changed.
