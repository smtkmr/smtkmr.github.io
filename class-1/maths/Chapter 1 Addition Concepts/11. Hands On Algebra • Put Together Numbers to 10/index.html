How many ways can we make 5?

Grandma has 5 flowers. How many can go in her red vase and how many in her blue vase?

Try a colour change

Change the groups. Keep 5.

Move the slider. One red cube changes to blue at each step.

All six ways

Math talk: How do you know you found all the ways?

Start with 5 red cubes and 0 blue cubes. Change one red cube to blue each time. Stop when all 5 are blue. We did not skip a step!

Question 22 · Find all the waysYour turn

I have 8 marbles. What could my groups be?

Find the seven ways with both colours. Fill in the missing blue numbers.

1 red7 red

7 + 1 = 8

No empty groups here. Both colours must be used.

Red marbles + blue marbles = 8

Short solution

The pairs are 7 + 1, 6 + 2, 5 + 3, 4 + 4, 3 + 5, 2 + 6 and 1 + 7. Each pair makes 8 and uses both colours. 8 + 0 and 0 + 8 do not fit this question.
