Activity 03 · Look a little closer

Spot the number!

Count only the black spots on each ladybug’s wings. Remember: no spots means zero.

Question 08Your turn

Count the black spots

Short solution

There are no black spots on the wings. No spots means 0.

Question 09Your turn

Count the black spots

Short solution

There are 2 spots on each wing: 2 + 2 = 4 spots.

Question 10Your turn

Count the black spots

Short solution

There are 3 spots on the left, 3 on the right and 1 in the middle: 3 + 3 + 1 = 7 spots.
