Math talk: Why is the sum the same across and down?

We add the same numbers. Only the way we write them changes. 2 + 4 makes 6 both ways. 3 + 2 makes 5 both ways.

Big question: Why are some addition facts easy?

Adding 0 leaves the number the same. Adding 1 means the next number. For other facts, start with one number and count on. Pictures can help too!
