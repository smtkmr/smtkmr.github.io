A little warm-up

Six tools for an addition explorer

Read these examples first. Then try the questions below.

🧩

1. Parts make a whole

2 + 1 = 3

2 and 1 are addends. They are the numbers we add. 3 is the sum. It tells how many in all.

🐾

2. Count on

5 + 2 = 7

Keep 5 in your head. Count two more: 6, 7. Stop at 7.

🫙

3. Zero adds none

4 + 0 = 4

No more objects join. The group stays the same. Adding zero keeps your number.

🔄

4. Swap the parts

2 + 4 = 6
4 + 2 = 6

Swap the addends. The sum stays the same.

🔍

5. Find a missing part

3 + ? = 5

Start at 3. Count up to 5: 4, 5. That is two more. The missing part is 2.

🟰

6. Equal means the same

3 + 2 = 4 + 1

Both sides make 5. The equals sign says the two sides have the same value.

👀 Look carefully🖐️ Use fingers or counters💬 Say your thinking🌱 Mistakes help us learn
