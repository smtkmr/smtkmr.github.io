Understand first

A sentence tells a complete thought.

These sentences tell who or what we are talking about and what they do. Start a sentence with a capital letter. These telling sentences end with a period.

Pronunciation & Hindi help

अ सेंटन्स टेल्ज़ अ कम्प्लीट थॉट।

वाक्य एक पूरी बात बताता है। जैसे: लड़का खेला।

“The boy” leaves us waiting. “The boy played” tells us what happened.
