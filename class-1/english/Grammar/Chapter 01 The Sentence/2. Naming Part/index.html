Look for who or what

Meet the naming part.

The naming part tells who or what did or does something. It can be one word or a group of words.

Pronunciation & Hindi help

द नेमिंग पार्ट टेल्ज़ हू ऑर वॉट डिड ऑर डज़ समथिंग।

वाक्य का naming part बताता है कि कौन या क्या काम करता है या उसने काम किया।

Who?Jasonwent to the circus.
What?A dogjumps through a hoop.

Ask: “Who went?” → Jason. Ask: “What jumps?” → A dog.

Writing wrap-up

Make your own circus poster.

Write a title and three sentences. Tell what people and animals do. Add pictures on paper, or choose a picture below. Read your poster aloud and ask someone to name each naming part.

Check your writing

Can you point to who or what in every sentence? Did you tell what they do? Start with a capital letter and end with a period. Many answers are possible. Review your work with a grown-up.
