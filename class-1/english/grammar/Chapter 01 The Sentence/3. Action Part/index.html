A sentence has parts

The action part tells what happens.

The naming part tells who or what. The action part tells what the naming part did or does. Keep the whole action part together—it may have more than one word!

A friendly pronunciation tip

Hindi letters below are sound hints. English sounds can be a little different, so listen to the audio and try saying each word aloud. Clap once for each word; do not add an extra “ed” sound unless you hear it.

Quick sound check

  1. Which word has a silent b: climbed or slides?
    Check your thinking

    Climbed. Say “क्लाइम्ड”; do not pronounce the b.

  2. Which word ends with a “t” sound: walked or showed?
    Check your thinking

    Walked. It sounds like “वॉक्ट.” Showed ends with a “d” sound.

  3. What does watched mean in Hindi?
    Check your thinking

    देखा. Example: “A chipmunk watched.” = “एक चिपमंक देख रहा था।”

  4. Say followed in the sentence: “Ken followed the signs.” What did Ken do?
    Check your thinking

    Ken ने संकेतों का पालन किया; “followed the signs” पूरा action part है।

  5. Which word tells an action happening now: slides or climbed?
    Check your thinking

    Slides tells what Ken does now. Climbed tells what Ken did before.

  6. Read aloud: “Anna walked on a log.” Which words tell what Anna did?
    Check your thinking

    Walked on a log. Keep the whole action part together.
