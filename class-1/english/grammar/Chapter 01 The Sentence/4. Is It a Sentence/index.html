Part 1: Lesson Content

पाठ की सामग्री

1. WHAT IS A SENTENCE? (वाक्य क्या है?)

A complete sentence has a naming part and an action part.

Say: ए कम्प्लीट सेंटेंस हैज ए नेमिंग पार्ट एंड एन एक्शन पार्ट।

Meaning: एक पूरे वाक्य में एक नाम बताने वाला हिस्सा (naming part) और एक काम बताने वाला हिस्सा (action part) होता है.

2. Key Parts of a Sentence (वाक्य के मुख्य भाग)

Naming Part (नाम वाला भाग)

Tells who or what the sentence is about (e.g., A big frog).

Say: टेल्स हू और वॉट द सेंटेंस इज़ अबाउट (ए बिग फ्रॉग्)।

Meaning: यह बताता है कि वाक्य किसके बारे में है (जैसे—एक बड़ा मेंढक).

Action Part (काम वाला भाग)

Tells what the naming part does (e.g., jumps off a rock).

Say: टेल्स वॉट द नेमिंग पार्ट डज (जम्प्स ऑफ ए रॉक्)।

Meaning: यह बताता है कि नाम वाला हिस्सा क्या काम करता है (जैसे—चट्टान से कूदता है).

3. Complete Sentences vs. Word Groups (पूर्ण वाक्य बनाम शब्दों के समूह)

Fish swim in the water. — Yes

Meaning: मछली पानी में तैरती है। (यह एक पूर्ण वाक्य है क्योंकि इसका पूरा अर्थ निकलता है).

Sleeps on a log. — No

Meaning: लॉग पर सोता है। (यह पूर्ण वाक्य नहीं है क्योंकि इसमें यह नहीं बताया गया कि कौन सोता है).

Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs 1 to 15)

Question 1Your turn

What does a complete sentence always have?

Question 1

Short Solution: A sentence must have both a naming part (who/what) and an action part (what happens) to give complete meaning.
Question 2Your turn

What is the Hindi meaning of "action part"?

Question 2

Short Solution: Action means work or action performed, so "action part" translates to "काम वाला भाग".
Question 3Your turn

In the sentence “A big frog jumps off a rock,” what is "A big frog"?

Question 3

Short Solution: "A big frog" tells us who the sentence is about, so it is the naming part.
Question 4Your turn

In the sentence “A big frog jumps off a rock,” what is "jumps off a rock"?

Question 4

Short Solution: "jumps off a rock" describes the action done by the frog, so it is the action part.
Question 5Your turn

Which of the following is a complete sentence?

Question 5

Short Solution: "Fish swim in the water." has both a naming subject ("Fish") and an action ("swim in the water").
Question 6Your turn

Which of the following is not a complete sentence?

Question 6

Short Solution: "Floats on the pond." lacks a naming part to tell what or who is floating.
Question 7Your turn

If a word group only says "Floats away," is it a complete sentence?

Question 7

Short Solution: No, because it missing the naming part, making it incomplete.
Question 8Your turn

Choose the complete sentence from these options:

Question 8

Short Solution: "The seeds open." has a naming part ("The seeds") and an action part ("open").
Question 9Your turn

What should you write after a complete sentence like “Plants grow.”?

Question 9

Short Solution: Write "yes" to confirm that it is a complete sentence.
Question 10Your turn

What should you write after an incomplete word group like “Deep water.”?

Question 10

Short Solution: Write "no" because "Deep water." lacks an action part and is not complete.
Question 11Your turn

Which group of words forms a complete sentence suitable for a sign?

Question 11

Short Solution: "Ducks live here." is a complete sentence containing both subject and action.
Question 12Your turn

Identify the naming part in the sentence: “The green frog.”

Question 12

Short Solution: "The green frog" is the complete noun phrase acting as the naming part.
Question 13Your turn

Which word group tells an action being performed?

Question 13

Short Solution: "jumps off a rock" contains the action verb "jumps".
Question 14Your turn

How do you pronounce "Naming Part" in Hindi approximation?

Question 14

Short Solution: Phonetically, "Naming Part" is pronounced as "नेमिंग पार्ट".
Question 15Your turn

Complete sentences always express a complete...

Question 15

Short Solution: Sentences express a complete thought or idea so that the reader understands the message fully.

Section B: Fill in the Blanks (Questions 16 to 30)

Question 16Your turn

A complete sentence has a naming part and an action ______.

Short Solution: The missing word is "part", completing "action part".
Question 17Your turn

In the sentence “Fish swim in the water,” the naming part is "Fish" and the action part is "______ in the water".

Short Solution: "swim" is the action verb performed by the fish.
Question 18Your turn

Read and copy each word group. Write yes after each complete sentence and ______ after the other word groups.

Short Solution: Write "no" for incomplete word groups.
Question 19Your turn

In the example, “The seeds open. ______”.

Short Solution: Write "yes" because "The seeds open." is a complete sentence.
Question 20Your turn

The word group “Plants grow.” is a complete sentence, so we write ______.

Short Solution: "yes" is written because "Plants grow." is full and complete.
Question 21Your turn

The word group “Floats away.” is not a complete sentence, so we write ______.

Short Solution: Write "no" as it lacks a naming part.
Question 22Your turn

The word group “Deep water.” is incomplete, so we write ______.

Short Solution: Write "no" because it misses an action part.
Question 23Your turn

The word group “It falls down.” is a complete sentence, so we write ______.

Short Solution: Write "yes" as "It" is the naming part and "falls down" is the action part.
Question 24Your turn

Ducks live here is an example of a complete sentence used to make a ______.

Short Solution: "sign" is the correct context word.
Question 25Your turn

“Children swim here.” is a complete sentence because it tells who is swimming and what they ______.

Short Solution: "do" completes the explanation of action.
Question 26Your turn

“Swims and runs.” lacks a naming part, so it is not a complete ______.

Short Solution: "sentence" is the term for a complete thought unit.
Question 27Your turn

“Little children and dogs.” lacks an action part, so we write ______ after it.

Short Solution: Write "no" because it has no verb or action part.
Question 28Your turn

“All boats stay out.” is a complete sentence, so we write ______ after it.

Short Solution: Write "yes" because it gives full sense.
Question 29Your turn

A naming part tells who or what the ______ is about.

Short Solution: The definition states that naming parts tell what the "sentence" is about.
Question 30Your turn

An action part tells what the ______ part does.

Short Solution: An action part shows what the "naming" part does.
