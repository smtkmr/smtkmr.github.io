Understand first · पहले समझें

A sentence tells a complete thought.

A sentence tells who or what we are talking about and what they do. It begins with a capital letter and ends with a period.

Key Concept 1 · मुख्य नियम

Two Parts of Every Sentence

Every telling sentence needs two parts working together:

  • Part 1: Who or What (कौन या क्या?) — The naming part (e.g., *The boy*, *The train*, *A balloon*).
  • Part 2: Did What / Action (क्या हुआ / क्रिया?) — What they do (e.g., *played*, *goes fast*, *popped*).
❌ Incomplete (अधूरा)

"The boy ..."

Leaves us waiting! (क्या हुआ? कुछ पता नहीं चलता)
✅ Complete (पूरा विचार)

"The boy played."

Tells us who and what happened! (लड़का खेला)

Key Concept 2 · लेखन के 2 सुनहरे नियम

Capital Letter & Period (.)

Always remember these two golden writing rules:

🔤 Rule 1: Capital Letter at the Start

वाक्य हमेशा बड़े अक्षर (Capital letter) से शुरू होता है। जैसे: The boy...

🛑 Rule 2: Period / Full Stop at the End

वाक्य के अंत में पूर्ण विराम (.) लगाया जाता है जो बताता है कि बात समाप्त हो गई।

Warm-up Example (मिस्टर पटर और टैबी):

"Mr. Putter cheered. Tabby purred and hiccupped."

(मिस्टर पटर ने खुशी से आवाज़ लगाई। टैबी ने घुरघुराया और हिचकी ली।)

👦 Sentence 101

The boy played.

बोलेंद बॉय प्लेड।

Why is this a sentence? (यह वाक्य क्यों है?)

The boy tells WHO (कौन). played. tells the action (क्या किया). It starts with capital 'T' and ends with a period.

🧱 Sentence 202

The blocks fall.

बोलेंद ब्लॉक्स फॉल।

अर्थब्लॉक (खिलौने के गुटके) गिरते हैं।

Why is this a sentence? (यह वाक्य क्यों है?)

The blocks tells WHAT. fall. tells what happens. Together they share a complete thought.

Grammar Checklist · याद रखने योग्य नियम

The Three Golden Rules of a Sentence

वाक्य के 3 सुनहरे नियम (Three Golden Rules)

1. Complete Thought: A sentence must make complete sense. It tells WHO and WHAT happened.
2. Capital Letter: Always start with a big, capital letter.
3. Period (.): Always end a telling sentence with a full stop.
