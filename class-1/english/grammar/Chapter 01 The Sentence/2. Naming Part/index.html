Grammar Foundation · आधारभूत नियम

Meet the Naming Part.

The naming part tells who or what did or does something. It can be a single word or a group of words.

Rule 1 · जादू का सवाल (Ask Who or What?)

Who or What does the action?

Whenever you read a sentence, ask this question to spot the naming part immediately:

Sentence: Jason went to the circus.

👉 Ask: "Who went to the circus?" → Jason (Naming part!)

Sentence: A dog jumps through a hoop.

👉 Ask: "What jumps through a hoop?" → A dog (Naming part!)

Rule 2 · एक शब्द या शब्दों का समूह (Whole Naming Part)

Keep the whole naming part together!

A naming part can be just one word, or several words that name someone together:

One word: Children clap. / Lions roar.

(केवल एक नाम शब्द: Children, Lions)

Group of words: The clown holds the hoop. / The red balloon floats.

(पूरा समूह: The clown, The red balloon — 'The' या 'A' को साथ रखें!)

Grammar Checklist · याद रखने योग्य नियम

Three Golden Rules for the Naming Part

नेमिंग पार्ट के 3 सुनहरे नियम (Naming Part Rules)

1. Ask Who or What: The naming part always answers "Who did this?" or "What happened?"
2. One Word or Group: It can be a single word (Jason, Children) or a whole group (The clown, Two small dogs).
3. Usually at the Front: In most telling sentences, the naming part comes right at the beginning.
