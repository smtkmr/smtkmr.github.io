📖 Read the complete story / पूरी कहानी

Today is 5th September. This day is celebrated as ‘Teachers’ Day’. Teachers and children are happy in the school. Meena, Rosy, Rohit and Anil are in the classroom. Mrs Goyal is their class teacher. She enters the classroom. “Happy Teachers’ Day,” shouts everyone in the class. The class teacher is happy to see sweets, biscuits, ice creams and fruits on the table. Rosy gives the teacher a bunch of flowers. “Thank you,” says the class teacher. She gives sweets, biscuits, ice creams and fruits to the children. The children have a class party. They are celebrating ‘Teachers’ Day’.

Good for life · जीवन की सीख

Respect. Gratitude. Learning.

Teachers’ Day is good for life as it teaches respect, gratitude, love for learning and honours those who guide us.

बोलें: टीचर्ज़ डे इज़ गुड फ़ॉर लाइफ़ ऐज़ इट टीचिज़ रिस्पेक्ट, ग्रैटिट्यूड, लव फ़ॉर लर्निंग ऐन्ड ऑनर्ज़ दोज़ हू गाइड अस।

अर्थ: शिक्षक दिवस हमें सम्मान, आभार और सीखने के प्रति प्रेम सिखाता है तथा हमारा मार्गदर्शन करने वालों का सम्मान करता है।

A little thank you matters.

We can thank our teachers with kind words, careful listening and a card we make ourselves.

हम प्यार भरे शब्दों, ध्यान से सुनने और अपने हाथों से बनाए कार्ड से शिक्षकों को धन्यवाद दे सकते हैं।
