Oh, why does the sparrow, build a nest in the rain? Won’t the downpour wash the nest away? Asked the child, to her grandmother. Monsoon is a time, when there are lots of worms, and that means food for the little ones! That’s why sparrows build a nest in the rain, said the grandmother with a smile.

— Chithra Padmanabhan

The rainy secret

Rain brings food.

In the poem, grandmother explains that monsoon brings plenty of worms—food for baby sparrows.

कविता में दादी या नानी समझाती हैं कि मानसून में बहुत सारे कीड़े होते हैं, जो गौरैया के बच्चों का भोजन बनते हैं।

Stay curious & kind.

Ask questions about nature. Watch birds quietly without touching their nests.

प्रकृति के बारे में सवाल पूछें। घोंसलों को छुए बिना पक्षियों को शांति से देखें।

The child worries about the nest washing away; the poem does not say that it actually happens.
